# Atmosphere direction

Reference research: Chilla's Art's official pages for [Night Security](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2367230/Chillas_Art_Night_Security/) and [The Closing Shift](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1843090/Chillas_Art_The_Closing_Shift/), including the former's store screenshots. The relevant goal is a believable ordinary Japanese place, consistent material scale and lighting, restrained presentation, and psychological anticipation. This is an interpretation for SAKIKO, not a claim to reproduce those games or reuse their assets.

Applied direction:

- Inner corridor walls are solid. A small selection of exterior room windows gives views outside; storage has none, toilets use narrow obscured panes, and most other panes are frosted or partly covered with blinds. Views are projected onto a distant plane at the source panorama's aspect ratio, rather than stretched across the window surface. Only two selected clear windows reveal the courtyard openly.
- Practical fluorescent pools alternate with dark stretches. The Mobile renderer enables real light cookies and light shadows; Compatibility silently ignored the old flashlight cookie. A dim broad spill surrounds the feathered core.
- Recorded female humming, laughter and screaming replace synthesized stand-ins for Sakiko's vocal cues. Position, attenuation, distance and quiet intervals create anticipation; early exploration, the old-wing threshold and key discoveries have distinct sound beats. There is no constant barrage of non-positional screams.
- The face keeps its original damaged skin texture. Hair conceals the back and temples, with a closed dark undercoat and fine strands. Pitch-black eyes have only a tiny muted catchlight, and the face and mouth use continuous matching boundaries instead of separate jaw/lip pieces.
- Gameplay remains readable and accessible. Reduced effects avoids extra distortion; screen-space UV warping stays removed.

The four-key story clues and exact ending prose are documented separately. Asset attribution is in ASSET_SOURCES.md.
